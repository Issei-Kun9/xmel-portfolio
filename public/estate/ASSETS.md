## How they were processed

- Models: 1k glTF from Poly Haven, then optimized with glTF-Transform and meshoptimizer.
  - Foliage cards were thinned to a random subset and enlarged slightly (island tree 7%; fir 5% near and 1.2% far), with alpha blend changed to alpha mask.
  - Solid meshes were simplified (rocks 12–25%, branches 8%).
  - Textures were re-encoded to WebP, and geometry was meshopt-compressed.
- Textures: 1k diffuse, OpenGL normal and ARM (AO/roughness/metal) maps, re-encoded to WebP.
- Skies: the 1k HDR is used for image-based lighting, and Poly Haven's tonemapped JPG (resized to 4096×2048 WebP) is used for the visible sky.

## Built for this scene (not external assets)

The house architecture (walls, slabs, glazing, mullions, stairs, jetty, pool), the terrain heightfield and all typography textures were authored in code for this site, using the PBR materials above.
No CC0 or otherwise freely licensed, web-ready luxury-villa model was reachable from the build environment. Sketchfab and the marketplaces require an account and carry per-model licenses.
