In India, customers would rather WhatsApp you than fill in a form or make a call. That makes WhatsApp your most important sales channel, and raises a question every growing business hits: is the free WhatsApp Business app enough, or do you need the WhatsApp Business API?

## The WhatsApp Business app

The free app you download from the Play Store or App Store.

**Good at:**
- A business profile with address, hours, catalogue and website
- Quick replies, greeting and away messages
- Labels to organise chats
- Free to use

**Limits:**
- Built for a person (or a small team) replying by hand
- Canned greeting and away messages, but no real automation or AI
- Can't connect properly to your CRM, calendar or lead sources
- Using unofficial tools to automate it violates WhatsApp's terms and risks your number being banned

**Right for:** businesses where the owner or one person comfortably answers every chat, quickly.

## The WhatsApp Business Platform (the "API")

Meta's official platform for businesses that need automation and integrations. You access it through Meta directly or a Business Solution Provider.

**Good at:**
- Automated replies to new enquiries in seconds, 24/7
- AI that can read the message, answer questions and qualify the lead
- Connecting to your lead sources (portals, website forms, ads), calendar and CRM
- Several team members handling one number
- Approved message templates for reminders and follow-ups

**Things to know:**
- Needs a dedicated number and business verification with Meta
- Conversations are billed per message by Meta; rates depend on the message type and country and change over time, so check Meta's current pricing
- Business-initiated messages outside the customer's 24-hour window must use approved templates
- Customers must be able to opt out, and you must honour it

**Right for:** businesses getting more enquiries than they can answer quickly, or getting them at hours when nobody's awake.

## The real question: how fast do you reply?

The app vs API choice is really about response time. If an enquiry at 10:47 PM gets answered at 10 AM, the customer has usually already booked with whoever replied first. Research on lead response is consistent: [the chance of converting a lead drops sharply within the first hour](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads).

Rule of thumb:

- **Fewer than a handful of enquiries a day, and you reply within minutes?** The app is fine.
- **Missing replies at night, on weekends or when busy?** The API with automated replies will win you more business.
- **Leads from portals or ads (99acres, MagicBricks, IndiaMART, Justdial, Facebook)?** The API lets you reply to every one within a minute, automatically. See [how to auto-reply to portal leads](/blog/whatsapp-auto-reply-99acres-magicbricks-leads).

## Moving from the app to the API

- The number used on the API can't be used on the regular app at the same time. Many businesses use a new dedicated number for the API, or migrate their existing one.
- Your business must be verified with Meta.
- Plan your templates (welcome, reminders, follow-ups) and get them approved.
- Decide when the AI hands over to a person: complaints, negotiations, anything unusual.
