"How much does it cost?" is the first question every business owner asks about AI lead response — and most providers make it hard to answer. This guide breaks down what you actually pay for, the typical price ranges, and our own published prices for the US and India, so you can compare like with like.

## What you're paying for

Every AI lead response system has three cost layers:

1. **Setup** — connecting your lead sources (portals, ads, website, phone), writing the conversation flows, integrating your calendar and CRM, and testing on real scenarios.
2. **Monthly** — hosting, monitoring, fixing things when a lead source changes, and improving the conversation as you learn what works.
3. **Usage** — the per-message and per-minute charges from the phone, SMS and WhatsApp providers, and the AI model itself. For most small businesses this is modest, but it scales with lead volume.

Beware of quotes that hide one of these. A low monthly price with no setup often means you do the setup yourself; a "flat" price may cap your minutes or messages.

## The main options

### Do-it-yourself tools

Missed-call text-back and auto-reply features built into CRMs and phone systems cost little or nothing extra. They send a fixed message, which is far better than silence — but they don't qualify the lead, answer questions or book appointments.

### Off-the-shelf AI receptionist apps

Subscription AI receptionists and AI texting tools are widely available, typically priced per month with limits on minutes or conversations. They're quick to start. The trade-off is fit: you adapt your process to the tool, and connecting less common lead sources — Indian property portals, for example — can be difficult.

### A human inside sales agent or receptionist

A full-time person gives you judgement and relationship skills, but costs a full salary, works set hours, and can't reply to three leads at the same instant. Many teams end up using AI for the first minutes and people for the conversations that need them — see [voice AI vs a human ISA](/blog/voice-ai-agent-vs-human-isa).

### A custom-built system

A system built around your lead sources, your questions and your calendar. It costs more upfront than an app, but it fits how you work and you aren't paying for features you don't use.

## How to judge the return

The maths is simpler than it looks. You need three numbers:

1. **Leads per month** — from all sources.
2. **Your close rate** — what share of leads become paying customers.
3. **Average deal value** — commission per sale, or revenue per job.

Then ask: if replying in under a minute instead of hours turned even a few more leads per month into customers, what would that be worth? For a real estate agent, one extra closed deal a year usually covers the cost many times over. For a home-service business, the question is how many extra jobs a month cover the monthly fee.

## Questions to ask any provider

- What exactly is included in setup, and what counts as extra?
- Are there limits on leads, messages or call minutes?
- Who pays for phone, SMS and WhatsApp usage, and at what rate?
- Can I see it working on my own leads before committing?
- What happens to my data and my phone number if I cancel?
