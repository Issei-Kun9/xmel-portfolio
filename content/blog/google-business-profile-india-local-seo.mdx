"Dentist near me", "AC repair Andheri", "salon in Koramangala": for local businesses in India, Google Maps is often the first place a new customer finds you, and the listing they see is your Google Business Profile. It's free, and most businesses leave it half-finished. Here's how to set it up properly.

## Why it matters

Your profile shows your rating, photos, timings, location, phone and a WhatsApp or website link, before anyone visits your website. For many customers, that's the whole decision. A complete, active profile with good reviews simply wins more calls than a bare one.

Google says local results depend on [relevance, distance and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). A strong profile improves the two you control.

## Step 1: Claim and verify

Search your business name on Google Maps. If a listing already exists, claim it; if not, create one at business.google.com. Verification is usually by phone, email or video. Use the business's own phone number and an email you'll keep.

## Step 2: Fill in everything

- **Business name**: your real name, exactly as on your signboard. Adding keywords like "Best Salon in Delhi" breaks Google's guidelines and risks suspension.
- **Primary category**: the most specific one ("Dental clinic", "Interior designer", "Air conditioning repair service").
- **Address and pin**: check the pin sits exactly on your entrance. Wrong pins cost walk-ins.
- **Service area**: if you visit customers (AC repair, pest control, interiors), set the localities you cover.
- **Hours**: including Sundays and festival closures.
- **Phone and WhatsApp**: a number that's actually answered.
- **Website**: even a simple one-page site helps credibility. See [what a website costs in India](/blog/website-cost-small-business-india).
- **Services and prices**: list your services, with "starting from" prices where you can.

## Step 3: Photos that sell

Upload real photos: your storefront (so people recognise it), inside, your team, and your work. For interiors and salons, before-and-afters are your best salespeople. Add new photos every month. Active profiles look alive.

## Step 4: Reviews, the right way

- Ask every happy customer, at the moment they're happiest (after the haircut, the handover, the treatment). A QR code at the counter or a WhatsApp message with the direct review link works well.
- Reply to every review, in the language the customer used, politely, even to the angry ones.
- Never buy reviews or post fake ones. Google removes them and can penalise the listing.

## Step 5: Keep it active

- Post updates or offers occasionally (festival offers, new services).
- Answer questions in the Q&A section before someone else does.
- Keep hours and phone current. A customer who arrives to a closed shutter won't come back.

## Step 6: Answer fast

A great profile brings enquiries: calls, WhatsApp messages, website forms. If they wait hours for a reply, they message the next listing. Businesses that reply in minutes win far more of those enquiries. An [AI assistant on WhatsApp](/blog/whatsapp-auto-reply-99acres-magicbricks-leads) can reply within a minute, answer the common questions and book the appointment, even at 11 PM.

## Common mistakes

- A pin that's a few buildings off
- "Open 24 hours" when you're not
- A phone number that goes unanswered
- Stock photos instead of real ones
- Ignoring negative reviews
