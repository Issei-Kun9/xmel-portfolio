## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Recording:** Use screen recording (OBS or phone) for the lead form + the AI chat response
- **Editing:** CapCut or Premiere — split-screen effect, timer overlay on both sides
- **Timer font:** Monospace (JetBrains Mono or similar) — keep it consistent with the site's terminal aesthetic
- **Color coding:** Left side (AI) gets green accent (#C1FF72), Right side (Human) gets muted red/orange
- **Pacing:** Each comparison beat should be 4–5 seconds — enough to read the text but fast enough to feel urgent
- **Music:** Keep it subtle. The text does the talking. A low lo-fi beat or trending "tech" audio works.
